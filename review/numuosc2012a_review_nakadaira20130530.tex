\item
{\em
  I glanced the reply for the comments. I felt that Hayato-san's comment
  (May.19) and my comments (May 22th) were not understood correctly.

  What we afraid is that the $\Delta\chi^2$ for 68\%CL (90\%CL) may not be 1
  (2.69) for this case. Because this relation between $\Delta \chi^2$ and
  confidence level is calculated assuming the fitter have the Gaussian response 
  without any boundary nor any bias.
  
  If that assumption is true, the $\chi^2$ curve should be the parabola function
  and the best fit point should be "the bottom of parabola".
  (In other words, the derivative of the $\Delta\chi^2$ should be zero at the best fit point.)

  Hayato-san and I are not simply discussing if the best fit is the
  $\chi^2$ minimum in the region of interest. We are discussing the shape of $\Delta\chi^2$
  curve around the best fit. It is different from the parabola function around the bottom.
  
  For the disappearance analysis, both VaLOR and KIT, those assumption is not applicable.
  The fitter has not Gaussian response due to the boundary.
  The fitter has non-negligible bias. And the best fit point is not the bottom of parabola. 
  
  So, it is difficult to say that the $\Delta\chi^2$=1(2.69) is corresponds to 68\%CL
  (90\%CL) without any proof, I think. 
  
  Please reconsider about the comments by Hayato-san and I.
