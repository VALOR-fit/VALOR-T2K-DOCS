\section{Summary of changes included in version 4 of T2K-TN-141 (released on Feb. 14, 2013)}

This section is designed to allow readers to keep track of the changes between versions of T2K-TN-141.
Table, figure, equation, footnote or citation numbers shown in black refer to version of 3,
{\color{red}whereas the numbers shown in red refer to version of 4}.
Both numbers are shown where possible.

\begin{itemize}

\item
Fix typo on line 179 {\color{red}(179)}) in which
$S_{m;t;r\prime;\vec{f}}$ was mistakenly $S_{m;r\prime;\vec{f}}$

\item
Fix typo on line 441 {\color{red}(441)}) in which $M_A^{QE}$ and
$M_A^{RES}$ were $f_{17; t, r}^{banff}$ and $f_{18; t, r}^{banff}$
respectively; they are in fact $f_{16; t, r}^{banff}$ and $f_{17; t,
  r}^{banff}$.

\item
Clarify what numbers we are quoting in Table 6 {\color{red}(6)}) for $f_{16; t, r}^{banff}$ and $f_{17; t,
  r}^{banff}$ using footnote {\color{red}(4)}). They are not the
\emph{value} of $M_A$, but $x_{MA}$ which is a multiplicative factor
describing how $M_A$ moves from the nominal ($M_A$ $\rightarrow$ $x_{MA} M_A$).

\item
Clarify the muon neutrino contour committee's requirements in section
5.6 {\color{red}(5.6)}.

\end{itemize}
