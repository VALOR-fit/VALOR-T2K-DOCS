\begin{abstract}

The results of the {\em VALOR}
3-flavour $\nu_{\mu}$-disappearance analysis on the combined Run1+2+3+4 dataset 
were reported in T2K-TN-183v1.
All comments received from the analysis review committee and the collaboration
are addressed, in detail whenever required, in this addendum.
Revisions of T2K-TN-183 made as a response to these comments 
are also summarized here.

\end{abstract}
