\section{Summary of changes included in version 6 of T2K-TN-141 (released on May 29, 2013)}

This section is designed to allow readers to keep track of the changes between versions of T2K-TN-141.
Table, figure, equation, footnote or citation numbers shown in black refer to version of 5,
{\color{red}whereas the numbers shown in red refer to version of 6}.
Both numbers are shown where possible.

\begin{itemize}

\item
  The limit on $\sin^2 2\theta_{23}$ in Section 5.4 {\color{red}(5.4)}
  is quoted with higher precision.
  Previously the limit was $\sin^{2}2\theta_{23}$ $>$ 0.95 at 90\%;
  the limit is now $\sin^{2}2\theta_{23}$ $>$ 0.958 at 90\%.
  The limit was rounded-off to 0.95, rather to 0.96, so that we are on the conservative side.
  However, we subsequently felt that it is better to quote 3 decimal points in the technical note
  and let the paper committee decide how to best present the limit in the PRL.

\end{itemize}
