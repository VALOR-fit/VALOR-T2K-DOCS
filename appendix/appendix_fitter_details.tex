\section{Implementation details}
\label{app:fitter_details}

% Ereco Vs Theta mu-like and e-like
As in the previous analysis \cite{OurRun17cCC1piJointAnalysis3Flavour}, the $e$-like samples have analysis templates that are built as a function of both the reconstructed neutrino energy and lepton angle, in order to improve the separation of the different reaction / flavour components, in particular $\bar{\nu}_{\mu} \rightarrow \bar{\nu}_e$ and $\nu_{\mu} \rightarrow \nu_e$, fundamental in measuring $\delta_{CP}$.
Furthermore the $\nu_e$ intrinsic beam and NC components can also be better separated from signal. The addition of the variable $\theta$ increases the number of template bins and so the CPU time, the number of reconstructed neutrino energy bins has not been reduced, in order to retain the previous sensitivity to oscillations. For $\mu$-like samples the templates are built as a function of the reconstructed neutrino energy only as in the past analyses.
