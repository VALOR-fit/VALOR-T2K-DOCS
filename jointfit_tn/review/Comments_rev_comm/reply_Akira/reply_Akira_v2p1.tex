{\Large \textbf{Comments on TN266v2.1 by Akira Konaka}} \\

- There are slightly different narrations on the CP results in this technote. Could we say that CP conservation assuming the PMNS framework is excluded at 90\%CL? \\
L1201: ``$\delta_{CP} = 0$ is still disfavored with respect to $\delta_{CP} = \pi$, which, however, 
is excluded at 90\% CL. We can say that CP conservation is excluded at 90\% CL.'' \\
L1217: ``With the Run 1-7 data set we still cannot exclude the hypothesis of CP conservation with a good significance.'' \\ 
L1266: ``The Run 1-7 data do not exclude the hypothesis of CP conservation, though $\delta_{CP} = 0$ is excluded at more than $2\sigma$
 and $\delta_{CP} = \pi$ is excluded at 90\% CL.''


\textcolor{blue}{
I modified the tree sentences in the text. Now I do not talk about ``good significance" which is quite subjective
but I only state the significance to which $\delta_{CP} = 0$ and $\pi$ are excluded and that
CP conservation with the PMNS model is excluded at 90\% CL.
