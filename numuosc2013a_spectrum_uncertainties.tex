\section {Updated estimates of the effect of systematic uncertainties in the prediction of the single-ring $\mu$-like event reconstructed energy spectrum}
\label{sec:spectrum_uncertainties}

In this section, we present the effects of the systematics described in Sec.\ \ref{sec:systematics}
on the expected number of single-ring $\mu$-like events and the reconstructed energy spectrum in SuperK.
These effects are shown both for no oscillations and for oscillations with
$\sin^{2}\theta_{23}$ = 0.5 and $|\Delta m^{2}_{32}|$ = 2.4$\times$10$^{-3}$ eV$^{2}$/c$^{4}$.
For the oscillation cases, the values shown in Tab.\
\ref{tab:pdg2012non23} are used for $\sin^{2}\theta_{13}$, $\sin^{2}\theta_{12}$ and $\Delta m^{2}_{21}$ 
and $\delta_{CP}$=0. The normal mass hierarchy is assumed.
All results are generated for an integrated exposure of 6.3933$\times$10$^{20}$ POT and
the BANFF-tuned MC templates are used.

The effects of systematic uncertainties on the expected number of events is summarised in Tab.\ \ref{tab:nsk_systematic_table_summary} 
both for no oscillations and for oscillations with the typical parameter values given above.
In this table, systematic parameters are grouped into a few categories and all parameter correlations are taken into account.
Entries for both the BANFF prefit and postfit errors are provided.
The effect of each parameter individually is presented in Tab.\ \ref{tab:nsk_systematic_table} in which the correlations
between parameters are necessarily ignored.

The effects of the combined systematic uncertainties on the reconstructed energy spectrum of single-ring $\mu$-like events
is shown in Fig.\ \ref{fig:1Rmu_error_envelope_total} for the typical oscillation scenario used.
This plot shows the total error envelope for the 1 $\mu$-like ring event reconstructed energy spectrum; this is calculated
as the $\pm$1$\sigma$ spread of bin contents from 100k toy MC experiments generated with randomized systematic parameters.
In Fig.\ \ref{fig:1Rmu_error_envelope_total}, all systematic parameters were considered and their correlations were taken
into account.

Error envelopes from the effects of certain groups of systematic parameters are shown in
Figs.\ \ref{fig:1Rmu_error_envelope_skdetfsi} (SuperK detector + FSI + SI systematics only),
\ref{fig:1Rmu_error_envelope_banff} (BANFF systematics only),
\ref{fig:1Rmu_error_envelope_uncor_xsec} (uncorrelated cross-section systematics only), and
\ref{fig:1Rmu_error_envelope_non23osc} (\emph{non-23-sector} oscillation parameters only).

Detailed studies showing the effects of individual systematics on the expected number of
single-ring $\mu$-like events are shown in \cite{OurRun1234DisappAnalysisSupplement}.
