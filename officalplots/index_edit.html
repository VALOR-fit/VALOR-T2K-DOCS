Important notes

These results have been superseded by results in the single angle \sin^2(\theta_{23}), available here

Octants

Octant 1 refers to \theta_{23} \leq 45 degrees, \theta_{23} \leq \pi/4, sin^2(\theta_{23}) \leq 0.5

Octant 2 refers to \theta_{23} \geq 45 degrees, \theta_{23} \geq \pi/4, sin^2(\theta_{23}) \geq 0.5

The difference between octants is due to the next-to-leading order term in:

P(\nu_{\mu}\rightarrow\nu_{\mu}) \approx 1 - \left( \cos^4(\theta_{13})\sin^2(2\theta_{23}) + \sin^2(\theta_{23})\sin^2(2\theta_{13}) \right) \sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta m_{31}^{2} L}{4E}\right).

Best fit information for octant 1(2)

\sin^2(2\theta_{23}) = 1.000(0.999)

\Delta m^2_{32} = 2.44(2.44) \times 10^{-3} eV^2 / c^4

\chi^2 / ndf = 56.04(56.04) / 73

Observed events @ SK = 58

Expected events @ best fit = 57.97(57.92)
