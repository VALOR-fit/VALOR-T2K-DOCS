Important notes

Primary result

It has been decided that contours made with the Constant delta-chisq method should be shown as the main result

It has been decided that contours made with \sin^2(\theta_{23}) (single angle) should be shown as the main result

Other results are available here

Best fit information

\sin^2(\theta_{23}) = 0.514\pm0.082

\Delta m^2_{32} = 2.44^{+0.17}_{-0.15} \times 10^{-3} eV^2 / c^4

\chi^2 / ndf = 56.03 / 71

Observed events @ SK = 58

Expected events @ best fit = 57.92

Expected events @ no oscillations = 205 \pm 17 (syst.)

The best fit point is close to the maximum amount of disappearance allowed by the 3-flavour model (non-zero \theta_{13} moved this away from \theta_{23}=\pi/4)

P(\nu_{\mu}\rightarrow\nu_{\mu}) \approx 1 - \left( \cos^4(\theta_{13})\sin^2(2\theta_{23}) + \sin^2(\theta_{23})\sin^2(2\theta_{13}) \right) \sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta m_{31}^{2} L}{4E}\right).

Total error envelope of the 1 $\mu$-like ring event reconstructed energy spectrum, for oscillations with $\sin^{2}2\theta_{23}$=1.0 and $|\Delta m^{2}_{32}|$=2.4$\times10^{-3}$ $eV^{2}/c^{4}$. The 2012 PDG values are used for $\sin^{2}2\theta_{13}$, $\sin^{2}2\theta_{12}$ and $\Delta m^{2}_{21}$, and $\delta_{CP}$=0. The normal mass hierarchy is assumed. The numbers shown were calculated for an exposure of 3.010$\times$10$^{20}$ POT. BANFF-tuned MC templates were used. The error envelope was calculated as the $\pm$1$\sigma$ spread of bin contents using an ensemble of 100k toy MC experiments generated with randomized systematic parameters. All systematic parameters were considered and their correlations were taken into account.
