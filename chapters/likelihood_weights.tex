Procedure: 
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Generate fake-data toy
	\item Randomise osc. \& syst. params
	\item The $\chi^2$ for the toy is calculated by summing over all samples (excluding RHC 1-ring e-like) and MC template bins $\chi^2 = \sum_{s \ne \bar{\nu}_e\textrm{-like}} \sum_{i} \chi^2_{s,i} \left( n^{obs}_{i} = 0, n^{exp}_{i} \right)$
	\item Do steps 1-3 for 2M toys and store the minimum $\chi^2$,  $\chi^2_{min}$
	\item Repeat steps 1-3 and calculate the likelihood weight for each toy $w = \exp{\left[-\frac{1}{2} \left( \chi^2 - \chi^2_{min} \right)\right]}$ until 2M toys have been accepted
\end{enumerate}

Where summing over $i$ denotes summing over all $E_{rec}, \theta$ bins in the MC template and the $\chi^2$ for each bin is given by:
\begin{equation} \label{eq:chisquare}
\chi^2_{s,i} = 
2 \cdot \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} 
\Big( 
n^{obs}_{i} \cdot \textrm{ln} (n^{obs}_{i}/n^{exp}_{i}) +
(n^{exp}_{i}-n^{obs}_{i}) 
\Big) 
\end{equation}

Criteria for accepting a toy:
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Calculate $w$
	\item Throw a uniform random number, $R$, from $\{0:1\}$
	\item If $w <R$, the toy is accepted
\end{enumerate}
