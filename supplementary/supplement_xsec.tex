\section{VALOR vs MaCh3 effects of systematics (prefit).}

The plots shown in this section use the prefit BANFF errors.
+1,+3,-1 and -3 sigma systematic variations are shown.
VALOR variations are represented by the thin continuous lines,
while MaCh3 variations are shown as thicker, dashed lines.

There are two small discrepancies, both of which are understood.
The figures relevant to these points are outlined in red.

\begin{itemize}
 \item Figure \ref{fig:syst_var_ereco_f_banffnorm_nuetonumu_rhc_valor_vs_mach3}.
  NuE/NuMu is inconsistent due to a small difference in the way oscillations are applied.
  VALOR applies oscillations per-bin. MaCh3 applies them per event.
  Note the very small scale of the effect on the y-axis.
 \item Figure \ref{fig:syst_var_ereco_f_sk_e_scale_rhc_valor_vs_mach3}.
  Energy scale shows small differences, again due to a difference in approach.
  VALOR applies this systematic per-bin. MaCh3 applies them per event.
\end{itemize}
