
\section{Additional $CC1\pi^{+}$ sample at Super-K}
\label{sec:nuecconepi_sample}
Another sample in addition to the four single ring samples used in the previous VALOR joint $\nu/\bar{\nu}$ fit analysis \cite{OurRun1617b17cJointAnalysis3Flavour} is included in the present analysis, this new sample is termed \nueCConepi{}. This is the sample included in the analysis which includes a non CCQE-like signal topology. The signal selection cuts allow events with one decay electron are included in the electron neutrino appearance sample, whereas the single $e$-like ring in previous analyses only allowed events with no decay electrons. This new cut introduces a large muon neutrino background, since the resulting decay electron ring may have been the result of a muon neutrino CCQE interaction and subsequent muon decay. The neutrino energy reconstruction is performed by modification of the CCQE prescription and assumes that the single pion in the final state is produced by an outgoing $\Delta^{++}$ baryon, without considering nuclear effects. Further sample selection details can be found in \cite{InelSglPionNueDecayE}.
